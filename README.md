# Learn Sketchnoting with Phoebe

Six 45-minute sessions on listening with a pen: the five shapes, the seven layouts, the
hierarchy that makes a page readable by somebody else, and the honest evidence on what
visual notes do and do not do.

**Live site:** https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-sketchnoting-with-phoebe/

| # | Session | Signature thing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ideas, not art | The drawing objection killed in ten minutes; three literatures kept apart |
| 2 | Five shapes, one vocabulary | Rohde's five elements; a twenty-icon vocabulary for your own domain |
| 3 | The selection bench | A talk plays at speaking pace; the bench measures what survived |
| 4 | Seven layouts, one page | Rohde's seven patterns; picking one before the talk starts |
| 5 | Readable by someone else | Hierarchy, the lettering ladder, containers; the thirty-second scan |
| 6 | Live, then shared | One real talk captured live; the one-page recap that leaves your desk |

The bench in session 3 (`assets/sn-live.js`) plays a real talk one word at a time and
computes, from what you typed, how many of its ideas survived, how much of your text is the
speaker's exact words (the one note-taking finding every replication reproduced), and what
each idea cost you. It measures selection, not drawing, and says so.

Every fact, its verification tier and the bench's honest limits:
`materials/official-course-map.md`.

Static HTML, CSS and JS. No build step. by Phoebe Fu.
